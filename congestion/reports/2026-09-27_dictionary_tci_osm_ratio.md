# Dictionary entry for tci_osm_ratio (plan v2, section 1)

27 September 2026, workstream B, Step 1. This extends the dictionary in `reports/waze_inventory.md`, which `01_inventory.R` regenerates, so the extension lives in this separate file. Sources are the provider documentation converted to `docs/waze_documentation.md` (line numbers below) and the data-auditor's first look at `Data/Waze/raw/roadlengths_quito.csv` of 2026-09-27. Items the documentation does not settle are marked **pending, provider**. Updated on 29 September 2026 with the provider's answers (`docs/2026-09-29_provider_answers.md`), rows "Averaging over days" and "Waze road length".

| Item | What the documentation says | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Numerator | TCI, the sum of the lengths of all jam lines observed in the cell during the window. Each jam line is counted once per feed observation in which it appears, so the unit is metres times observations (lines 139-143). | Settled |
| Denominator | OSM road length of the cell times the number of feed observations in the window: `tci_osm_ratio = TCI / (osm_roadlength × N_obs) × 100`, with `N_obs = datetime_group / feed_frequency`, for example 60 / 5 = 12 per hour (lines 170-172). Line 53 says jam lines are published "approximately every two minutes" but the pipeline uses a nominal 5-minute feed frequency. | Settled for the formula; the 2-minute versus 5-minute wording is a minor inconsistency in the documentation |
| Units and scale | Percent of the possible metre-observations that were congested, native 0 to 100 (line 181). -999 means reference length 0, and -998 means a network shorter than 150 m that would give a ratio above 100 (line 500). None occurs in the pre period (`Output/Waze/step1/panel_summary.md`). | Settled |
| Road types | all_roadtype is the union of all segments of the cell, not a speed class (lines 105 and 118). | Settled |
| Averaging over days | Hourly profiles are "averages by hour of day computed over all days of the period" (line 436). Provider answer 2 of 2026-09-29 (`docs/2026-09-29_provider_answers.md`): for each cell-hour, TCI is congested metres over the cell's total OSM metres; the monthly value for an hour (for example 9 a.m.) sums that hour over the month's business days, with TCI = 0 for cell-hours without a record, and divides by the number of times the hour occurs on business days. **So each delivered monthly value already averages over all business days, counting days without a jam as zeros.** The weekday restriction comes from the provider email of 17 September 2026 (plan section 1). | Settled (answer 2). Whether "días hábiles" also excludes public holidays is not stated |
| Road-network vintage | The documentation does not date the OSM snapshot. The road-length file gives annual OSM lengths that change from year to year in most cells, which suggests an annual vintage. | **Pending, provider** (which OSM version per year) |
| Overlapping jam segments | Segments are split across H3 cells in proportion to their length, "without double counting when a segment crosses several cells" (line 86). The worked example assumes non-overlapping jam lines (line 152). Nothing says how two simultaneous jam lines on the same stretch are counted. | **Pending, provider** |
| Quality filter | Summarized tables apply `flag_corr_type = 0` by default (line 494). The delivery has no `flag_corr_type` column (`reports/waze_inventory.md`, line 39), so whether the filter was applied cannot be checked. | **Pending, provider** |
| Waze road length (for tci_waze_ratio and the coverage screen) | Segments that appeared as a jam at least once in the year, never below the maximum of earlier years (line 67). Provider answer 3 of 2026-09-29: it is not the network Waze has mapped. For each month the provider deduplicates the jam segments reported and sums their metres per cell; the 2019 value is the maximum over the months of 2019, and each later value is the maximum of the previous value and the new months. **So the 2022 value used by the coverage screen measures the cumulative jam extent Waze had observed by 2022 (a running maximum since 2019), not road coverage.** The road-length file does not apply the documented cap at OSM length (line 69). | Settled (answer 3). The screen stays labeled "jam-derived coverage" |
