# Crash data delivery from the Agencia Metropolitana de Tránsito

- **From:** Dennys Mosquera, Coordinador General de Ingeniería de Tránsito y Seguridad Vial, Agencia Metropolitana de Tránsito (AMT), Municipio de Quito, on 2026-09-23. This answers Leonel's request of 2026-08-20 and his follow-up of 2026-09-17. Metro de Quito (Vanessa Rodríguez) was copied. The AMT's statistics unit prepared the matrix.
- **What was requested:** the record-level data behind the Quito Data Vial dashboard, for the whole district, from January 2020 to the latest month, with location, date and time, type, severity (injured and dead), vehicles involved and probable cause.
- **What arrived:** `REPORTE 2026-175 MATRIZ SINIESTRALIDAD DMQ 2021-2026.xlsx`, January 2021 to August 2026. See `docs/data_provenance/road_safety__2026-09-23_amt_siniestros.md` for the sheets, row counts and sha256.
- **Why 2020 is missing:** records for 2020 and earlier are not georeferenced and do not have the variables and detail now recorded through the Parte Único de Tránsito, so the provider advises against mixing them in.
- **Personal data:** license plates were removed for data protection.
- **Terms of use, from Leonel's request:** the data are used only for this evaluation; results are published in aggregate; the main findings will be shared with the AMT.
