## Model and reporting

For each distance band and outcome, fit monthly PPML:

`E[Y_it] = exp(unit_FE_i + month_FE_t + beta * Metro_i * Post_t)`.

Use all retained comparison discs in the pooled main model and one common post coefficient across stations. No synthetic control, seasonality term, trend control, power calculation, simulation or placebo test. Report `100*(exp(beta)-1)` and its **95% confidence interval**, obtained by transforming the log-coefficient interval. Cluster the sandwich standard errors by unit, using `fixest::ssc(K.adj=TRUE, K.fixef="nonnested", G.adj=TRUE, G.df="min", t.df="min")`: exclude unit effects nested in clusters from the parameter correction and use Student-t critical values with contributing clusters minus one degrees of freedom. Report contributing clusters and separation/omitted units; omissions are not a volume screen. All crashes within 500 m is the sole primary outcome; outer-ring and pedestrian results are descriptive, with pointwise intervals.

Produce **one quarterly event-study figure for all crashes**, overlaying the two bands estimated separately. Replace the common post regressor by treatment interacted with opening-aligned quarters (December–February, March–May, June–August, September–November), omitting September–November 2023. Retain monthly fixed effects and the same unit-clustered pointwise intervals. Mark opening and partial endpoint quarters. The main comparison is mostly the northern and southern periphery against the central spine. Pre-opening quarters are the specified check of whether that comparison is fair. Leads describe departures from proportional parallel trends, not proof of that assumption. Clustering permits within-area serial dependence but assumes cross-area independence; shared corridor shocks/spatial dependence can narrow intervals incorrectly. Do not interpret a confidence interval covering zero as evidence of no change.
