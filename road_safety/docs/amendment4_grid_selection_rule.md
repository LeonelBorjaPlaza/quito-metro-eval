# Amendment 4 comparison-grid rule, fixed before selection

Leonel approved Amendment 4 except its comparison units on 2026-10-07. This rule replaces the road-corridor comparison footprints only; the revised units await approval. Selection uses no crash data.

1. Use a square grid in WGS84/UTM 17S (EPSG:32717), anchored at easting/northing zero. Centres lie at integer multiples of 1,000 m. Radius is 500 m; disc interiors do not overlap. No alternative grid origin or spacing will be tried.
2. Retain whole discs wholly inside DMQ and entirely outside 1 km operating-station buffers and 500 m buffers around the 2022 Trolebús/Ecovía trunks. Do not clip discs. This implies centre-to-station distance strictly greater than 1,500 m and centre-to-trunk distance strictly greater than 1,000 m. Boundary cases are excluded.
3. Use the full January 2022 OSM motor-road network prepared by `19_amend2_geography.R`. Fast roads are motorway/trunk segments and their links, plus primary/secondary segments and their links on the existing named fast-road list: Simón Bolívar; Interoceánica/Oswaldo Guayasamín; Ruta Viva; Panamericana; General Rumiñahui; Córdova Galarza; E35; Intervalles; Mariscal Sucre. Ref E35 also identifies the road. Exclude names beginning PASAJE from name-based classification. Match normalized names; do not use street names in crash records. Restrict name matching to these major OSM classes to avoid misclassifying residential streets with identical names.
4. Urban streets are the retained motor-road classes other than fast segments and `track`. Urban-street density is clipped urban-street length in km divided by footprint area in km². Use the minimum exact density across the fifteen existing nearest-station 0–500 m footprints as the floor. Station footprints retain Amendment 4's nearest-station partition, including overlap resolution.
5. Retain a geographically eligible disc if its urban-street density is at least that fixed station minimum AND fast-road length is no more than 25% of its full motor-road length. The denominator includes every motor-road class in the prepared network, including tracks. Zero-road discs fail the density/share requirement. Road lengths count mapped carriageways separately, consistently for station and grid footprints.
6. Never use crashes, population, resulting sample size, effect estimates or forecast precision to select discs. Do not adjust the density floor, 25% cap, road definitions, grid origin or exclusions after seeing retained units. Population density is a descriptive audit only, using the previous uniform parish-area census approximation.

The script will save the exact station-derived floor and this rule before calculating grid road metrics. It will save all candidates and reasons for exclusion, retained geometry and the built-environment audit. No outcome is loaded, model fitted, simulation run or placebo tested in this revision.
