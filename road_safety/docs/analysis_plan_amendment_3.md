## Questions, units and outcomes

Estimate the change in recorded traffic crashes near operating stations. Assign each crash to its nearest entrance within 600 m in fifteen station areas; retain audited station-point fallbacks. Keep all 39 distant parishes, including valleys, individually in the main comparator; no volume exclusions.

A second specification uses “would-be Metro” road corridors: Av. de la Prensa from Labrador to La Ofelia; Panamericana Norte from La Ofelia toward Calderón; and Autopista General Rumiñahui on the proposed Los Chillos–El Ejido alignment. Buffer 2022 OSM traces by 600 m, subtract 2 km operating-entrance buffers and resolve comparator overlaps. The 2031 timing is supplied context; these are road proxies, not official alignments. Public connector segments and the southern DMQ road-end proxy are audited in `would_be_route_segments.csv`/`would_be_anchors.csv`. Map the northern phases separately; combine them for estimation and retain the southern unit. Report the north's own change against distant parishes as a feeder/spillover check. Secondary-buffer overlap does not remove main parishes.

**H1:** all-severity traffic crashes within 600 m, two-sided. Include only CHOQUES, COLISION, ROCE, ATROPELLO, ESTRELLAMIENTO, PERDIDA DE CARRIL and VOLCAMIENTO. Exclude ATIPICO and CAIDA DE PASAJERO; the reported coding increase is supplied context. **H2:** ATROPELLO within 300 m, directional alternative of an increase. Holm-adjust these two tests at familywise 5%. The overlapping tests are not independent.

Decompose estimates and signs, without additional confirmatory tests: vehicle–vehicle, pedestrian and single-vehicle mechanisms; any private car, motorcycle, or bus/heavy vehicle; weekday Metro hours versus other hours. Private cars are AUTOMOVIL/CAMIONETA with PARTICULAR service; heavy/bus is BUS or CAMION under the delivered taxonomy. Vehicle categories overlap. Omit misaligned October 2025 vehicle rows only from vehicle decompositions. Use fixed weekday 05:30–23:00 bands in all periods. The expected pattern is more pedestrian and drop-off-related crashes near entrances, fewer private-car crashes along arterials. Drop-off is an interpretation, not an identified category.

Station dose profiles use mean monthly validations over the complete delivered December 2023–May 2025 period, derived from the dated hourly workbook (`data/metro_validaciones/punto 3 Información BID v2.xlsx`), because the companion workbook contains station totals rather than monthly rows. Compute the dose in Stage B only; do not extrapolate to missing future validation months. Regress each station's mean post counterfactual count gap (observed minus fitted no-Metro counts, per month) on its validation dose, report the slope and its rank under 9,999 label permutations (seed 20261006) across all 15 stations. This descriptive permutation requires exchangeable dose labels, not independent stations; geographic sorting weakens that assumption. It is not a causal dose effect. Report its two-sided inclusive rank p-value and floor 1/10,000; it is outside the two-test family.

Main pre: January 2022–October 2023. November is dropped for rationing; October's final rationing days remain under the requested window. Main post: P1 December 2023–August 2024 plus P2 January 2025–August 2026, excluding September–December 2024. There are 29 planned post months; the daily calendar length is recorded in the Stage-A table. Refit P1/P2 separately. Describe December-aligned quarterly leads/lags (Dec–Feb, Mar–May, Jun–Aug, Sep–Nov), reference Sep–Oct 2023; mark absent/partial bins. Never slice a pooled effect vector.

Fit PPML with unit and time fixed effects, corridor-group × calendar-quarter seasonality, and one common treated-post coefficient across the fifteen stations. Aggregate effects by summing observed and fitted counterfactual counts; report count change and proportional change. Seasonal groups are Metro versus distant parishes in the main model, and Metro/North/South in the secondary model; columns absorbed by time effects are omitted. The secondary comparison reports estimates/signs, not additional confirmatory tests. Arterial decomposition freezes `output/amendment2/arterial_names.csv` (2022 north–south roads within 1 km), with 30 m geometry/name confirmation. A daily model additionally includes nearest-zone REMMAQ daily rainfall and zone × weekday effects. Missing complete-day rain uses gauge-specific pre-period monthly medians and a missing flag, retaining all crash dates. Monthly is main; daily is a sensitivity. Date effects absorb common holidays and weekdays; no unidentified holiday coefficient is added. Do not control for traffic, congestion, Waze or ridership in the crash model.

**Stage-A choice: monthly.** Main-window planning MDEs are 13.0% fall / 15.0% rise for monthly, versus 14.5% / 16.9% for daily, from 22 pre months / 29 post months or 669 pre days / 883 post days. Source: `output/amendment3_stage_a/precision_comparison.csv`. Fit both only on the pre-period. Form the treated-minus-pooled-comparator gap in count residuals divided by each group's fixed pre-period mean fitted count, retaining all-zero dates. Use its Bartlett long-run variance (daily lag 90, monthly lag 3), the pre lengths and planned post lengths to approximate the variance of a pre/post mean difference. Use 80% planning power and two-sided 2.5% size to convert normal-quantile shifts into falls/rises. Select the smaller H1 log shift; ties select monthly. This normal planning approximation is not validated placebo power or an inference test. Record daily lags 30/180 and monthly 1/6 as planning sensitivity, without changing the stated selection rule.

## Inference and reporting

The network is one intervention; stations are not independent inference units. For in-space placebos, freeze a pre-period-only contiguous composite registry using H1 counts: at least three crashes/month on average and no more than 20% zero months. Adequate parishes stay separate. Grow inadequate seeds and neighbours by largest count, DPA-code ties, using shared boundary lines; list isolated inadequate groups as placebo-ineligible while retaining every parish in estimation. Use the same registry for both hypotheses. Each placebo excludes its members and actual Metro areas from its donor pool and refits the specification.

Freeze outcome-specific pre-only eligibility: positive fitted mean and finite positive residual-gap scale, with H2 also requiring pedestrian records in at least two pre months. These checks use pre-period fits only. Never drop a place on its post effect; a later undefined fit/scale makes that test unavailable, not zero or a silent exclusion. Current K is 13 for H1 and 12 for H2 (raw floors 1/14 = 0.0714 and 1/13 = 0.0769); both raw floors exceed Holm's first cutoff 0.025. Thus no five-percent familywise rejection or informative adjusted bound is attainable under this registry, regardless of the analytical MDE.

Rank the coefficient divided by its fixed pre residual-gap scale against similarly standardized placebo coefficients: absolute magnitude for H1, signed upper tail for H2. Inclusive p-values are `(1 + count at least as extreme)/(K + 1)`; report K, rank, floor and Holm adjustment. The minimum jointly adjusted p-value is 0.1429. Locations were not randomized; conditional comparability, including geographic dependence and different road exposures, is a substantive assumption.

For candidate log effects `(theta1, theta2)`, replace each observed standardized coefficient by `(beta_h - theta_h)/scale_h`, holding the fitted placebo coefficient reference fixed. Apply Holm to the two candidate p-values; retain pairs for which neither null is rejected, then project this joint accepted set onto each effect axis and transform by `100*(exp(theta)-1)`. This conditional reference set is not a validated randomization or Rambachan–Roth interval. With this registry it is the whole admissible effect space, so report “adjusted bound uninformative,” not a precise null. Never force finite or connected limits. Benchmarks are approximately +14% injury crashes near new stations, pedestrian-driven (He, An and Hu 2026), and +14% crashes on strike days (Bauernschuster, Hener and Rainer 2017); settings and outcomes differ.

Sensitivities: January 2021 start; omit January–March 2024; ATIPICO added back; injury-or-fatal crashes; and continuation of the pre-period relative-gap slope. Use the monthly count-gap slope (relative residual gap × pre mean count) with ±2 HAC slope-error range, lag 3, as planning sensitivity rather than validated coverage. Calendar time advances through omitted months. If Δt is the difference between mean post/pre calendar months, the sign-flipping slope is monthly H1 count change/Δt; report its excess over and multiple of the pre slope, with zero/undefined cases. Prior differential steps remain an identification limitation. This follows Rambachan and Roth (2023) in spirit, not their formal robust interval.

All shown counts round to five, suppress positive 1–4 and complements, and protect overlapping margins; exact inputs stay local and ignored.
