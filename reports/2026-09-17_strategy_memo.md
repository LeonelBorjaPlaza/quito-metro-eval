# Quito Metro congestion module: strategy options for author review

17 September 2026. This memo proposes choices; it does not lock an analysis plan. No new post-opening effect, ATT, event-study coefficient or synthetic-control/SDID fit has been computed. The authors' approved descriptive conventions govern the completed work. Estimation must wait for their plan and the data-quality decisions below.

## What the module inherits

The authoritative paper is Borja Plaza and Quintero, *Underground Relief: The Air Pollution Effects of Quito's First Metro Line*, dated May 31, 2026. Its preferred augmented synthetic-control specification includes monitor fixed effects and meteorological controls. At Centro, the PDF reports PM2.5 log effects of -0.130, -0.132 and -0.164 for the pre-disruption, donut and full windows, respectively: -12.2%, -12.4% and -15.1%, with conformal p-values 0.011, 0.041 and 0.006. With Belisario treated and Centro excluded, the corresponding log effects are -0.062, -0.024 and +0.140: -6.0%, -2.3% and +15.1%, with p-values 0.729, 0.849 and 0.698. These are existing paper results, not estimates from this module. [PDF, Table 5](../docs/paper/Underground-relief.pdf).

The pre-disruption gas results at Centro are CO -0.056 log points, -5.4%, p=1.000; NO2 -0.064, -6.2%, p=0.967; and SO2 -0.025, -2.5%, p=0.187. The paper distinguishes the well-fit NO2 null from uninformative CO results. Do not say every gas is null in every window: SO2 is +13.1%, p=0.055 in the donut window and +10.0%, p=0.124 in the full window. The separate spatial-placebo exercise reports Centro -12.2%, p=0.012 and Belisario +8.5%, p=0.452; that exercise is not the Belisario specification above. [PDF, Tables 6 and 7](../docs/paper/Underground-relief.pdf).

Phase A found agreement between PDF and CSV point estimates at displayed precision in the preferred local specification, but not a reliable mapping of exported p-values or SDID window labels. Conflicts P1 and P3 remain author items. The May 31 PDF postdates the export and supplies every paper estimate quoted here. CSVs remain available only for trajectories and donor weights; nothing in the frozen air-quality analysis has been changed or re-estimated. [Historical reconciliation](00_source_checks.md).

The paper interprets the localized particulate response as destination-based mode substitution and explicitly leaves direct traffic untested. Its weekday 07:00-09:00 and 17:00-19:00 windows appear in section 3.2 and Table 3. Waze lacks weekdays, so this module tests all-day-of-week congestion at those hours, not the exact pollution sample. The metro opened Friday, December 1, 2023. The module's monthly P1 is December 2023-August 2024, disruption is all of September-December 2024, and usable P2 is January plus May-December 2025. The deliberate monthly September assignment differs from the paper's weekly calendar. Waze P2 is longer than the paper's six PM2.5 weeks through March 2025 and 24 gas weeks through June 2025.

## Evidence that constrains the choices

The delivery contains 15,080,765 raw rows but 5,546,323 distinct cell-month-hour-roadtype keys. Exact full-row deduplication leaves 1,314,213 all-road keys and 852,218 fast-road keys. The saved blocks under Data/Waze/parquet/ preserve source multiplicity and supply all subsequent Waze reads. Singletons concentrate by cell: 250 all-road cells have only singleton keys, while the other observed cells have triplicates. Monthly and hourly singleton proportions stay near 14%. This is a spatial reweighting problem, not evidence of extra observations. All 2,469 polygon IDs and boundaries pass H3 resolution-8 validation. [Inventory](waze_inventory.md), [descriptives](waze_descriptives.md).

After the approved exclusions, the fixed population contains CENTER 7, BELISARIO 7, CORRIDOR 34, RING 56 and REST 1,533 cells. REST loses all 740 never-observed cells and 92 additional cells without January 2022-November 2023 records. Of eligible REST cells, 271 average at least 20 delivered hourly slots per pre month; 555 meet threshold 12. The completed congestion panel treats absent records as zero within usable months, but leaves February-April 2025 entirely NA. Jam-speed ratios and free flow remain undefined without records. This zero-completion convention is not proof that an unrecorded road had no congestion.

Two new findings make a provider review important before causal work. All 2,272 negative auxiliary OSM spread-ratio keys lie in REST; 294 belong to saturated donors. In 2,032 usable adjacent-month comparisons, flagged primary TCI has a median signed excess of 1.2367 percentage points over its two neighboring months. Unflagged rows have a median excess of 0.0159. Among 912 flagged rows with unflagged comparisons in the same cell-hour, 86.4% exceed their own reference median scaled absolute deviation. The primary values look potentially contaminated, although these diagnostics cannot prove where the error originates. Also, 8,290 fast-road keys across 280 cells lack an all-road key at the same cell-month-hour, contradicting simple row-support nesting. These findings weaken an unconditional absent-row-equals-zero interpretation. The descriptive conventions remain unchanged; no silent repair has been made. [Flag comparison](../Output/Waze/descriptives/negative_spread_comparison.png), [class support](../Output/Waze/descriptives/fast_road_support.png).

## 1. Treated unit and Belisario analog

A single monitor cell would align most closely with the pollution location, but the supplied monitor coordinates are rounded to 0.01 degrees, coarser than one cell. Drop this option for the current delivery; reinstate it only with precise, dated coordinates. The seven-cell neighborhoods used in Phase C explicitly represent uncertainty around each published point, not a verified monitor footprint. Their IDs appear in the descriptive report, with seeds 8866d33885fffff for Centro and 8866d33aa3fffff for Belisario. All fourteen cells have pre-period records, and both neighborhoods have complete peak record presence throughout the 23-month pre period.

Cells intersecting a UNESCO historic-center polygon would target the destination mechanism more directly, but no such boundary was supplied. It remains a conditional option, not an implementable specification today. It also needs an explicitly comparable Belisario footprint, such as a contiguous, equal-area neighborhood around its precise monitor point; choosing that footprint after viewing results would defeat the comparison. A station buffer around San Francisco is implementable now, but targets access to the station rather than the monitor or historic center. Its Belisario analog could use Iñaquito, the layer's nearest station, or a monitor-centered buffer. Those are different hypotheses and must be named before fitting. Centroid inclusion and any-intersection inclusion also differ and require an explicit choice.

I recommend the two seven-cell published-point neighborhoods as the provisional confirmatory units, with a station-buffer sensitivity fixed before estimation. They give symmetric, observed supports without pretending the monitor coordinates are precise. If a dated UNESCO boundary and precise monitor points arrive before the plan is locked, reconsider the main geometry on substantive grounds. Do not select geography using post-opening patterns. Station and monitor coordinates must continue to come from the supplied GPKGs. [Group map](../Output/Waze/descriptives/map_groups.png).

## 2. Donor pool and coverage

All 1,533 eligible REST cells maximize geographic breadth but include many sparse, low-congestion profiles. Their mean peak presence is 0.4377 in 2022, 0.4529 in January-November 2023 and 0.5222 in usable 2025. Conditional TCI also rises, from 1.9404 to 2.4026 to 2.6026 percent. Thus their delivered trend reflects both presence and recorded intensity; the decomposition cannot label the former penetration or the latter true traffic. Saturated REST presence is much steadier at 0.9854, 0.9784 and 0.9782, while conditional TCI is 3.8292, 5.0676 and 6.1975 percent. The saturated trend is largely an intensity change. [Presence-intensity decomposition](../Output/Waze/descriptives/rest_decomposition.png).

Pre peak correlations with the broad and saturated REST means are 0.9347 and 0.9454 for CENTER, and 0.9254 and 0.9342 for BELISARIO. These high level correlations do not remove substantial level differences: the pre combined-peak CENTER mean is 11.3010 percent, compared with much lower donor means. With only 23 pre months, an unrestricted search among hundreds of donors can overfit. Coverage selection also favors busy roads and changes the population to which the comparison applies. [Pre comparisons](../Output/Waze/descriptives/pre_donor_comparison.png), [saturated-donor map](../Output/Waze/descriptives/map_saturated_donors.png).

Matching REST on road length and roadtype composition could improve comparability, but no direct road-length field or functional road class was delivered. Speed-class support is not a substitute. Similarly, matching altitude and population density is unavailable without cell-level layers; monitor elevations cannot fill that gap. Drop these two matching options from the executable current-data menu, while retaining them as future inputs. Every donor option must first exclude CENTER, BELISARIO, CORRIDOR and RING; a coverage or matching screen must never admit the held-out ring. The current ring is centroid-based distance 1-2 km from a station, not distance from the continuous line or a proven no-spillover boundary.

I recommend the 271 saturated donors as the starting pool, with threshold 12 and the full eligible REST pool as prespecified sensitivities. Require pre-only held-out prediction checks, regularization and transparent weight concentration diagnostics. The negative-ratio flags affect even saturated donors, so coverage does not certify quality. Prefer provider correction before estimation. If correction is unavailable, the authors must choose a flagged-cell/row sensitivity and a missing-value rule; deleting flagged rows and silently zero-filling them would create another artifact. Do not restrict donors on favorable post trends or retrospectively increasing coverage.

## 3. Pre-period start and construction

January 2019 offers 59 pre months, including a pre-pandemic baseline, but requires a model that handles the 2020 collapse, recovery and construction years. The raw CENTER peak series averages 6.8767 percent in 2019, 3.6454 in 2020 and 8.2534 in 2021. These are different traffic environments. The common 2020 trough appears in both target neighborhoods and donor series, not merely in CENTER. January 2022 offers 23 months closer to the operating environment but less information for seasonality, validation and inference. CENTER's peak mean rises from 10.0063 in 2022 to 12.7134 in January-November 2023, while several comparators rise too.

I recommend January 2022 for the main pre period and January 2019 as a demanding stability sensitivity, not automatically as more useful sample size. The construction plot shows local and common variation, but no supplied dated closure log identifies a San Francisco or La Alameda teardown signature. A dip shared across groups is not evidence of a center-specific closure. Request construction and road-reopening dates; if they imply a late-pre structural change, authors should choose between a shorter stable window, explicit sensitivity windows, or postponing a causal claim. Do not pick the start that yields the strongest post effect. [Full trajectory](../Output/Waze/descriptives/series_tci_osm_ratio.png), [construction inspection](../Output/Waze/descriptives/construction_preperiod.png).

## 4. Hours, outcomes and the night diagnostic

A single peak outcome gives one clear confirmatory test with equal weights on hours 7, 8, 17 and 18. Separate morning and evening tests can detect an asymmetric travel response but add multiplicity. A full 24-hour vector is useful for exploration, yet 24 correlated outcomes do not create 24 independent monthly observations. The pre CENTER means are 10.8528 percent in the morning and 11.7492 in the evening; BELISARIO is 9.1775 versus 22.7642. Combining hours can conceal materially different neighborhood profiles. I recommend combined peak as primary, morning and evening as prespecified secondary decompositions, and the full profile as exploratory. Confirm bin endpoints and timezone with the provider; weekday-specific delivery would align better with the paper. [Hour profiles](../Output/Waze/descriptives/hour_profiles.png).

Retain tci_osm_ratio as primary, tci_severe_osm_ratio and avg_jam_speed_ratio as secondary, and tc_spread in metres as an indirect reporting/traffic diagnostic. Do not restore t_speed_ratio: the advertised identity S=100-T+TJ/100 fails by up to 4.170018 percentage points in the delivered aggregates, so exact redundancy is not a valid explanation for dropping it. Upstream averaging is a possible explanation, not a demonstrated one. Severe classification remains unresolved between Waze levels 3/4 and speed below 40% of free flow. Conditional jam speed measures a selected set of jams and can change when mild jams disappear, even without improved unconditional speed.

The approved night block is 0-4. CENTER's pre night primary mean is only 0.0265 percent, versus 11.3010 at peak, and night record presence is 0.7391 rather than 1. It is a useful falsification diagnostic, but a weak low-level outcome and not a guaranteed zero-effect placebo. Travel scheduling, late trips and road changes can affect night traffic even without metro service then. Hours 1-2 also overlap the documented free-flow reference. I recommend labeling night results diagnostic, never using a night null to validate the entire design or adjusting daytime outcomes by post-opening night measurements.

## 5. Estimators and inference

Augmented synthetic control is the closest transfer of the paper's local design: aggregate each target neighborhood first, then predict it from pre-screened REST cells or comparable donor neighborhoods. Ridge augmentation can reduce bias from imperfect pre matching, but permits extrapolation and therefore needs tuning, weight disclosure and pre-only validation. This is the methodological role of augmentation, not evidence that it fits these data well. [Ben-Michael, Feller and Rothstein](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.04170).

My recommendation is augmented synthetic control as the proposed primary local estimator, conditional on resolving the delivery problems and passing held-out pre checks. With 271 candidate donors and 23 monthly pre observations, use blocked validation and a constrained complexity search rather than an unconstrained high-dimensional fit. Estimate Centro and Belisario against the same eligible distant pool. Neither neighborhood should enter the other's donor pool in the confirmatory traffic design, even though the preferred pollution specification retains Belisario. The two separately estimated responses and their contrast require joint inference because controls and shocks overlap. A significant Centro estimate and an insignificant Belisario estimate alone do not demonstrate a significant difference or Belisario equivalence to zero.

SDID combines unit and time weighting in a panel estimator and offers a distinct robustness comparison. [Arkhangelsky et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.09970). Treat the neighborhood aggregate as one exposed unit, or define a genuinely pooled exposed region; seven adjacent cells are not seven independent treatment assignments. A local SDID robustness fit could use the same donors and outcomes, but the paper's citywide SDID with external city donors does not transfer automatically to a single Quito grid. Missing delivery months must be removed consistently, with calendar gaps retained in dependence assumptions. Neither a large number of hexagons nor time weights repairs spillovers or omitted measurement changes. No such fit was run here; augsynth and synthdid also lack compatible binaries in the approved repository.

Conformal inference can operate on counterfactual prediction errors; exactness depends on exchangeability conditions, while serially dependent settings require additional conditions and appropriate permutations. [Chernozhukov, Wuthrich and Zhu](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.09089). I recommend precommitting a dependence-aware conformal procedure and reporting its finite monthly support, rather than importing the paper's weekly p-values or treating months as freely exchangeable. A placebo-in-space exercise should preserve footprint and eligibility, refit under the same rules, disclose poor-fit exclusions, and use spatial blocks or nonoverlapping neighborhoods. The 271 cells are spatially dependent; a nominal rank resolution of 1/272 is not a claim of 271 independent controls.

A fake opening in 2022 would test whether recovery, construction or reporting shifts generate apparent effects before the real opening. It would require an earlier training period and hence confront the 2020-2021 instability that the 2022 main start avoids. It would not validate post-opening parallel trends or exclude 2024 confounding. The limited power exercise below instead uses a January 2023 fake opening with a 2022 baseline, wholly before the real opening.

An exploratory event-study DiD could describe dynamic exposure gradients if the plan fixes treatment geography and comparison units first. Cell and month effects, a common opening date, pretrend diagnostics, spatial dependence and familywise/FDR treatment of many cells must be explicit. It could reveal heterogeneity obscured by an aggregate, but cannot transform within-Quito controls into an unexposed city counterfactual. I recommend it only as a clearly exploratory secondary design, not an alternative significance search. No post-opening coefficient has been calculated in this session.

## 6. Disruption and estimands

The approved adaptation excludes all September-December 2024 from clean-window estimation. Keep that rule. Plan nine P1 months and nine usable P2 months, with all February-April 2025 outcomes missing for every cell. The paper's three estimands map to P1 alone, P1 plus usable P2, and the full available window including disruption but still excluding the delivery gap. P2 alone is an additional persistence summary, not a replacement for the paper's donut estimand. I recommend P1 as confirmatory, the combined clean window as persistence evidence, and P2 alone and the full window as clearly labeled secondary results. Equal-month weighting gives the two usable clean blocks equal weight; any duration or calendar weighting needs author approval. Missing months cannot be recovered by weighting or interpolation.

Keeping disruption months in the primary test would mix metro exposure with rationing, generators, wildfire-related travel changes and possible reporting disruptions. It is defensible only for a different all-events estimand, not as a cure for reduced power. The disruption shading does not itself prove a traffic effect of the crisis. The full-window option should assess sensitivity, with no causal interpretation assigned to the disruption's own raw movements.

## 7. Threats and design safeguards

Free flow does not divide primary TCI. Its direct denominator contains OSM road length and nominal observations. Free flow nevertheless affects which jams are detected, which jams qualify as severe, the speed ratios, and monthly speed-class composition. CENTER's all-hours delivered free-flow diagnostic is 41.1820 km/h in November 2023 and 39.4011 in December; it is 47.1863 in August 2024 and 43.0984 in September. Earlier volatility and differing donor movements preclude assigning these discontinuities to the metro. All-road primary TCI avoids the direct speed denominator and changing fast-road restriction, but not detection changes or changes in OSM/reference normalization. Fixed-segment and fixed-reference provider recalculations would be stronger safeguards than controlling for a potentially affected post-opening free-flow series. [Free-flow diagnostic](../Output/Waze/descriptives/freeflow_diagnostic.png).

Saturated donors reduce the extensive-margin coverage mismatch, but neither presence nor TCS identifies Waze penetration. Direct counts, expected feed coverage, missing-versus-zero flags and the explanation of nonnested class support are needed. A fixed pre-period coverage screen avoids conditioning on post outcomes. The never-observed exclusion uses whole-delivery existence as explicitly directed; document that choice and retain a future sensitivity based solely on pre-existing cells if it could alter the target population. Here the two target neighborhoods already have full peak pre support.

Construction teardown can change road access precisely where destination substitution is hypothesized. Buffer and UNESCO options change exposure to this threat but do not eliminate it. Closure logs and stable-pre-window checks are more informative than relabeling it a metro effect. The ring guards against nearby spillovers but cannot prove REST is untouched, especially along the connected road network. Wider buffers and spatially separated donors are possible plan sensitivities, at a cost in comparability and sample support.

The energy crisis motivates the fixed disruption exclusion; it does not guarantee that effects cease in January 2025. The separate P2 summary and full-window sensitivity make persistence assumptions visible. If the metro changes night traffic, it can also change the monthly free-flow reference and daytime classification. That makes the night test informative about measurement pathways but invalid as an automatically unaffected control. Finally, the flagged REST rows and missing all-road keys make provider correction or explicit robustness rules a prerequisite for a strong causal claim, not an optional cosmetic cleaning step.

## 8. Rough pre-only power

The reproducible calibration uses only January 2022-November 2023 primary peak values, read with an explicit date restriction from the cleaned Parquet. It subtracts the equal-weight saturated REST mean from CENTER without optimizing synthetic-control weights. CENTER's pre mean is 11.301003 percentage points; the residual SD is 1.370248. Circular-block resampling generates a hypothetical 23-month baseline and nine-month evaluation. For a two-sided 5% test with 80% simulated rejection under a constant reduction, block lengths 1, 3 and 6 give approximate minimum detectable reductions of 1.486, 1.681 and 1.852 percentage points. The length-3 value is 14.87% of the pre mean, not an estimated metro effect. An independent-normal reference gives 1.509360 points. [Calculation and assumptions](waze_preperiod_power.md).

The separate spatial stress calibration treats each of the 271 saturated donor cells as a historical placebo, contrasting January-September 2023 with a 2022 baseline against the other donors. It rescales shifts by each cell's baseline residual SD and CENTER's baseline residual SD. This gives a rough detectable reduction of 3.757 percentage points, 33.24% of CENTER's pre mean. It transports one-cell placebo noise to a seven-cell target and retains spatial dependence, so it is not an exact randomization power calculation. The gap between calibrations is evidence of design uncertainty, not a confidence interval for power.

I recommend planning around an optimistic serial-noise scale near 1.7-1.9 points and a conservative historical-placebo scale near 3.8 points, then recalibrating after pre-only estimator selection. Twenty-three months cannot validate seasonality, dependence, construction stability and high-dimensional weights with much precision. The contiguous nine-month simulation also differs from usable P2's interrupted calendar. No post-opening outcome enters either calculation, no post-opening fit was used, and no air-quality percentage was translated into a traffic effect.

## 9. Three possible tiers after the plan is locked

The confirmatory tier would use the chosen Centro and Belisario footprints, one primary peak TCI outcome, a shared distant donor rule and P1 as its primary window. It would report both neighborhood estimates, their joint contrast, pre fit, weight concentration, dependence-aware inference and specified persistence/sensitivity windows. Secondary severe congestion and conditional jam speed would illuminate the traffic pattern without replacing a failed primary test. The comparison tests whether the destination-centered prediction distinguishes Centro from an equally near noncenter location; it does not assume Belisario is untreated or that pollution and congestion effects must have equal percentages.

The citywide tier needs a choice of claim. With this Quito-only delivery, a true average citywide metro effect lacks an external untreated comparison if the metro influences the entire road system. I recommend dropping that causal estimand for the current data. A prespecified global null test across local exposure-defined responses is possible, with a joint spatially dependent statistic and multiplicity control, but it is a global test of differential patterns, not a citywide causal mean. If authors want the paper's citywide SDID analog, request comparable Waze panels for external donor cities and revisit the design before fitting. A descriptive citywide aggregate alone cannot supply the missing counterfactual.

The exploratory tier would map estimates or exposure-gradient summaries only after locking eligibility, geometry and outcome definitions. It should show missing/flagged cells, coverage and uncertainty alongside values, distinguish unsupported regions from estimated nulls, and correct for the family of tested cells/hours. Pre-period clustering or regional pooling could reduce noise and preserve spatial interpretability. An uncorrected map of cellwise significance would invite a discovery claim the design cannot support. The current maps show groups and coverage only; they contain no estimated effects.

## Contradiction and artifact register

Paper conflicts P1 (window-specific conformal p-values versus an older unlabeled export) and P3 (SDID labels/inference) remain unresolved author items. The weekly/monthly calendar mismatch is resolved operationally by the authors' deliberate monthly rule, not by changing the paper. Waze's longer P2, weekday mismatch and new three-month delivery gap remain explicit comparability limits. The CSV's extra serialized index is not an analysis variable; the one-time count and sampled-value comparison passed.

Delivery conflicts include monthly rather than weekly frequency; missing day-of-week, counts, flags and direct road-length fields; exact spatially concentrated duplicates; 740 empty cells and 92 additional pre-empty REST cells; the wholly missing March and suspicious February/April delivery; speed-ratio identity failure; fractional negative auxiliary ratios and severe persistence above 100; possible primary contamination on negative-spread rows; and nonnested fast/all row support. The severe definition conflicts within the documentation. The documented two-class split also omits exactly 40 km/h. large is a speed class, and free flow is not the direct primary-TCI denominator. These are retained as data-supported facts or unresolved definitions, not silently harmonized.

Spatial conflicts include an empty station shapefile versus 15 GPKG stations; San Francisco's layer coordinate (-0.2215025001, -78.5152698004) lying 146.1 m from the approximate prompt point; rounded monitor coordinates; nine monitor features including El Camal versus the paper's eight-station sample; and HubDist measuring distance to the nearest station rather than the continuous line. All delivered polygons intersect the requested box, but 117 centroids lie outside it; the exact selection rule remains unconfirmed. GPKG CRS and unused Z/M dimensions require the documented in-memory transformation. Upstream spatial-layer versions, UNESCO geometry, cell altitude and population density are missing. The observed 2020 trough, 2022-2023 growth, local free-flow volatility, night/peak differences and speed-class support changes are descriptive features, not assigned causes. The construction signature remains unverified without logs.

Environment limitations also survive handoff: Arrow had a mixed-package shutdown crash, h3jsr cannot load because its V8 dependency lacks libnode.so.127, and augsynth/synthdid are unavailable from the approved binary repository. DuckDB and its H3 extension worked throughout. Resolving later estimator installations may require author approval for another route; this session installed nothing further.
