# The paper's tables and numbers on the new data: what changes

Workstream A, priority 1. The local tables of "Underground Relief" (May 31, 2026 draft) are rebuilt from the committed step 2 outputs (`606ea82`), in the paper's layout. Every number in the text is listed with its old and new value. Nothing in the paper has been edited; this is for review.

## Result

Eleven passages cite an estimate whose sign changes or whose p-value crosses 0.05 or 0.10. They are listed below by line of `congestion/docs/paper/Underground-relief.txt`, and only these are flagged.
- **Donut and full PM2.5 windows.** Eight of the eleven passages concern them, because their p-values rise above 0.05: M8b donut from 0.041 to 0.199, M8b full from 0.006 to 0.097, and the same for M5b.
- **Placebos and SO2.** Two concern the spatial placebo (Belisario and Cotocollao) and one concerns SO2.
- **Unchanged.** The pre-disruption estimate and its significance do not change: 0.130 becomes 0.131 log points, still 12.2 percent, with p from 0.011 to 0.016.

## How it was checked

`code/local/step2/paper_tables.py` builds every table twice, from the frozen outputs and from the new ones.
- **Against the PDF text.** The frozen build is compared row by row with the printed rows in the text copy, and it reproduces every row of Tables 3, 5, 6, 7, A.1 and A.3. The one exception is the known Table A.3 typo (M8 donut printed -0.154; the source is -0.153).
- **Phrases.** Each mapped text phrase is checked to appear on its line.
- **Review.** Per your decision, the script was not code-reviewed, and this note did not go through the claims-auditor.
